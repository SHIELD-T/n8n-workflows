# DAY 101: Portfolio Finalization
**Week:** 15 — Graduation  |  **Time:** 2-3 hours  |  **Difficulty:** Advanced

## 🎯 What You'll Learn
- How to pick 3-5 projects (not all 50+ workflows you've touched) that actually make the strongest case for your skills
- How to publish a real, public-facing portfolio instead of a folder of JSON files only you can see
- What a hiring manager or prospective client actually scans for in the first 10 seconds of a portfolio

## 🎥 Watch First
[N8N FULL COURSE 5 HOURS (Build & Automate Anything)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7WsbtZwOx_U)
Watch with an eye toward which demonstrated capabilities look most impressive to an outside viewer — that's your selection filter today.

## 🛠️ Build It: Step-by-Step
1. From everything you've built this course, shortlist 3-5 projects that together cover different skills (e.g. one AI agent system, one business-automation workflow, one integration-heavy pipeline) — variety beats volume.
2. Pick a real, public home for the portfolio: a GitHub repo with a README, a Notion public page, or a simple personal site — choose one and actually set it up today.
3. For each of the 3-5 projects, write: a one-line hook (what it does and why it matters), the tools/nodes used, one real screenshot, and a link to the exported JSON (sanitized of any real credentials or client data).
4. Write a short portfolio intro (3-4 sentences) stating your specialization — tie it to your industry track from Week 6 rather than "I do automation" generically.
5. Add a real contact method (email, Calendly link, or contact form) so someone impressed by the portfolio can actually reach you.
6. Publish it and open the live link in an incognito/private browser window to confirm it's actually accessible to someone who isn't you.
7. Import `WEEK_15_GRADUATION/EXAMPLES/portfolio_finalization_system.json` and compare its project-organization structure to your own — check you're not missing an obvious section (skills summary, testimonials placeholder).

## 🔑 Credentials Needed
An account on whichever platform you publish to (GitHub, Notion, or a hosting provider) — free tier is sufficient.

## ✅ Definition of Done
- [ ] Your portfolio is live at a real, shareable URL — not a local file or private doc
- [ ] It features 3-5 projects with real screenshots, not placeholder images
- [ ] It states your industry specialization specifically, not generically
- [ ] You verified it's accessible in a private/incognito browser window

## 🐛 Common Pitfalls
- **Including every single workflow you ever built** — a portfolio with 50 mediocre entries reads worse than one with 4 strong ones; curate ruthlessly.
- **Screenshots of the n8n canvas with no context** — a screenshot alone means nothing to someone outside the course; always pair it with what problem it solved and for whom.
- **Portfolio never actually published, just "ready to publish"** — the live URL is the deliverable; a finished draft sitting in a private document doesn't count.

## 🏭 Industry Track Application
Your portfolio intro and project selection should make it obvious within 10 seconds which industry you serve — a HealthTech-focused portfolio should look meaningfully different from an E-commerce one, not just swap one word in a generic template.
