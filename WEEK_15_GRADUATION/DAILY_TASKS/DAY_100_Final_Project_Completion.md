# DAY 100: Final Project Completion
**Week:** 15 — Graduation  |  **Time:** 2-3 hours  |  **Difficulty:** Advanced

## 🎯 What You'll Learn
- How to pick your single strongest piece of work from 15 weeks and finish it to a genuinely presentable standard
- The gap between "it runs on my machine" and "someone else could run this," and how to close it
- How to write a project writeup that a stranger (recruiter, client, collaborator) could understand in under 2 minutes

## 🎥 Watch First
[n8n FULL COURSE 6 HOURS (Build & Sell AI Automations)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2GZ2SNXWK-c)
Skim for anything about presenting or packaging finished automations — that's today's actual job, not building something new.

## 🛠️ Build It: Step-by-Step
1. Review every workflow you've built across all 15 weeks (check each week's `EXAMPLES/` folder and your own saved exports) and pick the ONE that best demonstrates real skill — not the flashiest, the one that actually works end-to-end.
2. Open it in n8n and run it fully, start to finish, with realistic test data. Fix anything that errors — this is your final project, it needs to actually work today, not "mostly work."
3. Clean it up using what you learned on Day 79 (templating) and Day 95 (QA checklist): remove leftover test data, add Sticky Notes explaining each major section, verify the error branch is real and tested.
4. Export the final JSON and save it in a clearly named file (e.g. `final_project_[your_name]_[workflow_name].json`).
5. Write a one-page project writeup: the problem it solves, who it's for, the n8n nodes/integrations used, one real screenshot of a successful execution, and one sentence on the hardest part to build.
6. Record or write a 60-90 second "walkthrough" script you could actually deliver live — trigger in, what happens at each stage, what comes out.
7. Import `WEEK_15_GRADUATION/EXAMPLES/final_project_completion_system.json` and compare its structure/documentation style to what you just finished — borrow anything you're missing.

## 🔑 Credentials Needed
Whatever your chosen final workflow requires (commonly OpenAI plus one or two of: Airtable, Google Sheets, Slack, Gmail).

## ✅ Definition of Done
- [ ] Your chosen workflow runs successfully end-to-end today, with a real execution you can point to
- [ ] It's been cleaned up per your Day 95 QA checklist standard
- [ ] You have a real one-page writeup with a real screenshot, not placeholder text
- [ ] You have a 60-90 second walkthrough script you could deliver live right now

## 🐛 Common Pitfalls
- **Picking the most complex workflow instead of the most reliable one** — a flashy workflow that fails half the time is a worse final project than a simple one that works every time; reliability beats ambition here.
- **Writeup written for someone who already knows your course** — write it so a recruiter or prospective client with zero course context understands the value in the first two sentences.
- **Skipping the actual re-run** — "I know it worked before" isn't the same as running it today; confirm it still works, since credentials and external APIs change.

## 🏭 Industry Track Application
Choose your final project from your specific industry track if at all possible — a finished, working [your track] automation is a far stronger portfolio piece than a generic demo, since it signals real domain expertise to that track's buyers.
