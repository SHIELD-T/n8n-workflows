## 🛠️ Build It: Step-by-Step
1. Do a final live check of everything you're about to call "done": your Day 100 final project runs, your Day 101 portfolio URL loads, your Day 104 playbook URL loads, your Day 105 accountability workflow's last execution succeeded. Fix anything broken — nothing ships broken on graduation day.
2. Write your real, public course-completion post: what you built (name the actual final project), one specific number if you have one (workflows built, hours automated, a real client outcome), and a direct link to your portfolio.
3. Publish that post on the platform where your actual target audience is (LinkedIn for career/client-facing, a relevant community for peer visibility) — today, not "this week."
4. Consolidate every link from this week into one place: portfolio (Day 101), business doc (Day 102), resume/LinkedIn (Day 103), playbook (Day 104), and 12-month plan (Day 105) — a single "start here" document or page linking to all five.
5. Send that consolidated link to at least one real person outside the course (a potential client, a mentor, a peer) with a short note asking for honest feedback.
6. Write yourself one final note, dated today, to be reopened in 90 days per Day 105's reminder: what "success" will concretely look like by then, in your own words.
7. Import `WEEK_15_GRADUATION/EXAMPLES/graduation_achievement_celebration_system.json` and `WEEK_15_GRADUATION/EXAMPLES/graduation_achievement_system.json`, run both once, and confirm they still execute — a fitting final verification that what you leave behind actually works.

## 🔑 Credentials Needed

## ✅ Definition of Done
- [ ] Every link you're publishing (portfolio, playbook, final project) was live-checked today and actually works
- [ ] You published a real, specific completion post publicly, not a private draft
- [ ] You have one consolidated "start here" link covering portfolio, business doc, resume, playbook, and future plan
- [ ] You sent that consolidated link to at least one real person and asked for honest feedback

## 🐛 Common Pitfalls
- **Publishing a completion post before verifying the links it references still work** — a broken portfolio link in your graduation post undermines the entire post; check everything live first.
- **A completion post with no specifics** — "I finished an automation course!" gets scrolled past; naming the real final project and a real number gets read.
- **Treating graduation as the finish line instead of the handoff to Day 105's accountability system** — the 90-day note in step 6 exists specifically so today isn't where the momentum stops.

## 🏭 Industry Track Application
Your completion post and consolidated link should lead with your industry track specialization first — "I build automation systems for [your track]" is a stronger opening than "I completed a 15-week automation course," and it's the line that actually attracts the right opportunities next.
