# DAY 103: Career Finalization
**Week:** 15 — Graduation  |  **Time:** 2-3 hours  |  **Difficulty:** Advanced

## 🎯 What You'll Learn
- How to translate 15 weeks of coursework into resume/LinkedIn language a non-technical hiring manager or client would actually understand
- Why "completed an n8n course" is weak positioning compared to naming specific, provable outcomes
- How to build one real outreach list and send the first message today instead of "getting ready to" apply

## 🛠️ Build It: Step-by-Step
1. Decide which path you're actually pursuing right now: employed automation role, freelance/agency (your Week 11-13 business), or both — your finalization work today should match the real path, not a generic "career update."
2. Rewrite your LinkedIn headline and About section (or resume summary) using 2-3 specific, provable outcomes from this course — "built and shipped 5+ production n8n workflows including a multi-agent AI system" beats "completed automation training."
3. Add your Day 101 portfolio link prominently to your LinkedIn/resume/website — verify the link actually works by clicking it from a different device or browser.
4. Build a real target list: 10 real companies (if job-seeking) or 10 real prospects (if freelancing) you'd actually want to work with, using what you know from your industry track.
5. Draft one real outreach message template you'd send to this list — specific, not a generic "I completed a course" cold message — and personalize + send at least one today.
6. Import `WEEK_15_GRADUATION/EXAMPLES/graduation_achievement_celebration_system.json` and check whether it surfaces any career-related achievement framing worth reusing in your own positioning.

## 🔑 Credentials Needed
LinkedIn account access; email or the platform you're using for outreach.

## ✅ Definition of Done
- [ ] Your LinkedIn/resume headline names specific, provable outcomes, not generic course completion
- [ ] Your portfolio link is live and verified working from a second device/browser
- [ ] You have a real 10-name target list relevant to your actual path (employment or freelance)
- [ ] You sent at least one real outreach message today, not just drafted one

## 🐛 Common Pitfalls
- **Positioning that undersells specific, real work** — "learned n8n" is weak; "built a multi-agent AI orchestration workflow and a client-facing SLA monitoring system" is what actually gets attention.
- **Target list of vague categories instead of 10 real names** — "marketing agencies" isn't a target list; 10 named, real companies or people is.
- **Drafting the outreach message but not sending it** — today's Definition of Done requires at least one real send; a perfect unsent draft doesn't move anything forward.

## 🏭 Industry Track Application
Your target list should be entirely within your industry track if you're going the freelance/agency route — a focused list of 10 real prospects in one vertical converts better than 10 scattered names across unrelated industries.
