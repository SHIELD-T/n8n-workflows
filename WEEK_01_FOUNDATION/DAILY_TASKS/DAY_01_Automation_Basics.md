**Week:** 1 — Foundation  |  **Time:** 2-3 hours  |  **Difficulty:** Beginner

## 🎯 What You'll Learn

- How to set concrete personal/professional/business automation goals

## 🛠️ Build It: Step-by-Step
Today is about surveying your own world for automation opportunities and setting up your workspace — there's no n8n build yet (that starts Day 2), but you will produce a concrete artifact.

1. Open a blank document (Google Docs, Notion, or plain text) titled "My Automation Opportunities."
2. List 10 real, repetitive tasks from your work or personal life (e.g., "I manually copy leads from a form into a spreadsheet every day").
3. For each task, write three lines: **Current process** (what you do manually today), **Proposed automation** (what a trigger→action workflow would look like), **Impact** (time saved or errors avoided).
4. Pick 5 companies (e.g., Zapier, Shopify, HubSpot, Slack, Airbnb) and write one sentence each on what they automate and what tool/approach they likely use.
5. Create your "Automation Goals" doc with three sections — Personal, Professional, Business — and list at least 2 goals under each.
6. Join the course community (Discord/Slack) and post a short introduction that includes your automation goals from step 5.

**Stuck?** There's no example workflow for Day 1 — this is a planning day. If you want a preview of what you'll be building, skim `WEEK_01_FOUNDATION/EXAMPLES/manual_trigger_to_api_request.json` in a text editor (don't worry about understanding every field yet).

## 🐛 Common Pitfalls
- **Too vague:** "Automate my emails" isn't actionable. Rewrite it as "Auto-sort incoming invoices into a Finance folder and Slack me a summary."
- **Skipping the impact line:** If you can't state the time/error savings, the opportunity probably isn't well understood yet — dig one level deeper.
- **Waiting to start n8n:** Resist the urge to jump ahead and install n8n today; Day 2-4 walk you through this properly so you don't hit avoidable setup issues.
