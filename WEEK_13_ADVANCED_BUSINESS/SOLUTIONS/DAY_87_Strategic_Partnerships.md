# Day 87 Notes: Strategic Partnerships

*This day's deliverable is a written partnership proposal, not an n8n workflow — see `DAILY_TASKS/DAY_87_Strategic_Partnerships.md` for the full lesson.*

## Model Answer

**Target partner:** A local CRM consultancy already selling HubSpot implementations to SMBs.
**Partnership type:** Channel/referral.
**Value proposition:** They sell the CRM, you handle the n8n automation layer connecting it to email/calendar/invoicing — neither of you has to learn the other's stack.
**Ask:** 20% referral commission on any client you bring them, and the reverse.
**First outreach line:** "I noticed you implement HubSpot for [industry] clients — I build the automation layer around CRMs like that and think there's a referral fit both ways."

### 5 Real, Named Candidates

| Candidate | Type | What's in it for them |
|---|---|---|
| Local HubSpot consultancy | Channel/referral | Extends their offer without hiring an automation dev |
| Airtable's partner program | Technology/integration | Co-marketing to their SMB user base |
| A specific local business consultant | Strategic alliance | Adds a technical delivery arm to their advisory offer |
| A web design agency | Channel/referral | Upsell automation to clients who already trust them |
| A bookkeeping/accounting firm | Channel/referral | Offers clients invoice/reconciliation automation as an add-on |

### If They Say Yes — 3-Step Plan

1. First call agenda: confirm mutual referral terms, define handoff process.
2. What's needed from them: a simple referral-tracking sheet, an intro-email template.
3. 60-day success check: count of referrals sent each direction, revenue attributed to the partnership.
