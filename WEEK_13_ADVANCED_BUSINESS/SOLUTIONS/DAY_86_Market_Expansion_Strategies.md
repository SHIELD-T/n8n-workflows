# Day 86 Notes: Market Expansion Strategies

*This day's deliverable is a written market-entry plan, not an n8n workflow — see `DAILY_TASKS/DAY_86_Market_Expansion_Strategies.md` for the full lesson.*

## Model Answer

**Target market:** Healthcare automation (patient intake, appointment reminders, claims processing)
**Market size:** ~$2.5B globally, 15% annual growth — but sized down to a realistic reachable segment: local/regional healthcare clinics, not the global figure.
**Entry strategy:** Partner with 2-3 mid-size healthcare IT consultancies rather than selling direct — they already have HIPAA-compliant client relationships.
**Key risk:** HIPAA compliance for any patient data touched by a workflow — n8n self-hosted with a BAA-covered host is a prerequisite, not an afterthought.
**First move:** One pilot workflow (appointment-reminder automation) with one existing healthcare contact, priced at cost to get a case study.
