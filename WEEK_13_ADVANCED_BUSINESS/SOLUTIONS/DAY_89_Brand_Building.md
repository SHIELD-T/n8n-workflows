*This day's deliverable is written brand positioning and copy, not an n8n workflow — see `DAILY_TASKS/DAY_89_Brand_Building.md` for the full lesson.*

## Model Answer

### Positioning Statement

"For small e-commerce brands drowning in manual order/inventory work, [Your Name] Automation is the automation partner that builds workflows you can actually understand and edit yourself — unlike agencies that hand you a black box you can't touch without calling them back."

**Target audience:** Small e-commerce brands
**Differentiator:** Transparency and hand-off, not lock-in

### Brand Voice Traits

| Trait | Example sentence | Anti-example |
|---|---|---|
| Plain-spoken | "This workflow saves you 5 hours a week — here's exactly how." | "Our proprietary synergistic automation paradigm..." |
| Technical but accessible | "It checks your inventory every hour and texts you if stock drops below 10." | "Leverages event-driven microservice orchestration." |
| Honest | "This is a training project, not a paid client result." | "Guaranteed 10x ROI, just like all our clients!" |

### Visual Identity

- **Colors:** `#1B4B43` (deep teal), `#F2A65A` (warm amber accent), `#FAFAF7` (off-white background)
- **Fonts:** Headings — Space Grotesk; Body — Inter

### About Page Copy (draft)

"We build automation that small business owners can actually own. No black boxes, no lock-in — just workflows that save you real hours every week, documented clearly enough that you (or anyone on your team) can open them up and understand exactly what's happening. If you're an e-commerce brand still copying order data by hand or manually chasing inventory counts, that's exactly the kind of work we automate first. Book a free 15-minute audit and we'll show you where the time is leaking."
