# DAY 86: Market Expansion Strategies
**Week:** 13 — Advanced Business  |  **Time:** 2-3 hours  |  **Difficulty:** Advanced

## 🎯 What You'll Learn
- How to size a new market realistically instead of citing a generic "$X billion globally" figure that doesn't tell you anything actionable
- How to pick one adjacent vertical to enter based on real overlap with what you already know how to build
- How to turn "we could expand into healthcare" into a specific, resourced entry plan with a real timeline

## 🛠️ Build It: Step-by-Step
1. List every industry vertical you've touched so far in this course (via your industry track and any client/demo work) plus 2-3 adjacent ones you haven't.
2. For each of 3 candidate verticals, write down: what specific automation problem you'd solve, one real competitor or existing solution already serving that space, and why a business there would pick you over the status quo.
3. Score the 3 verticals on a simple 1-5 scale across: your existing expertise, market accessibility (can you actually reach buyers there), and competitive intensity. Add up scores instead of picking your gut favorite.
4. For your highest-scoring vertical, write a one-page entry plan: first 3 target customer profiles (specific enough to name real companies/roles), your entry channel (cold outreach, existing network, content), and a 90-day timeline with 3 concrete milestones.
5. Draft the actual first outreach message or content piece you'd use to enter this market — a real, sendable/publishable draft, not a description of one.
6. Import `WEEK_13_ADVANCED_BUSINESS/EXAMPLES/market_expansion_partnership_system.json` and compare its vertical-entry structure to your one-page plan — note anything you're missing (competitive analysis, localization/compliance considerations).

## 🔑 Credentials Needed

## ✅ Definition of Done
- [ ] You scored 3 real candidate verticals on a consistent scale, not a gut pick
- [ ] Your chosen vertical has a one-page entry plan with named target customer profiles, not generic personas
- [ ] You have a 90-day timeline with 3 specific, checkable milestones
- [ ] You drafted one real outreach message or content piece ready to send/publish

## 🐛 Common Pitfalls
- **Market size numbers copied from a generic source with no bearing on your actual reachable market** — a "$2.5B market" means nothing if you can only realistically reach 50 companies in year one; size your addressable market, not the global one.
- **Choosing a vertical because it sounds impressive rather than because you can serve it** — score honestly on your existing expertise; a vertical you know nothing about needs a much longer timeline than your scoring might assume.
- **Entry plan with no first outreach artifact** — a plan you haven't started executing (even in draft form) is still just an idea; step 5 exists specifically so you leave today with something real.

## 🏭 Industry Track Application
Use your actual industry track's adjacent vertical as one of your 3 candidates (e.g. if you've been building for E-commerce, score Retail/DTC brands as a natural adjacent market) so this exercise plugs directly into work you've already done this course.
