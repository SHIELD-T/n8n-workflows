## 🛠️ Build It: Step-by-Step
1. Re-open every workflow you built this week (Days 58-63) and run each one once more to confirm they still work — fix anything that broke.
2. Write a one-page summary (in a doc or Notion page) listing: the 6 authentication methods you configured, the 2 form integrations, the email/calendar automation, the Notion automation, both bots, and the complete system from Day 63.
3. Pick your strongest workflow from the week and clean it up: rename nodes clearly, remove test/debug nodes, and add a couple of sticky notes explaining what it does and what credentials it needs.
4. Export that cleaned-up workflow as JSON and save it somewhere you can find it later — this is the start of your personal portfolio, which you'll formalize in Week 11.
5. Take screenshots of your Week 9 system running successfully (execution log showing a full green run) for your own records.
6. Write a short list (3-5 items) of anything from this week you don't yet feel confident about — you'll have a chance to shore this up before Week 10 gets harder.

## ✅ Definition of Done

## 🐛 Common Pitfalls
- Skipping the re-test step — credentials expire, test webhooks reset, and a workflow that worked on Monday may silently be broken by Sunday
- Cleaning up a workflow by only renaming nodes but leaving debug/test nodes wired into the main path
- Treating "review day" as passive re-watching instead of actively re-running and fixing your own builds
