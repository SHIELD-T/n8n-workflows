## Model Answer

### Lane Automation Co. — Home Services Pricing Table

| Tier | Price | Timeline | Deliverables (outcome-framed) | Payment terms |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | $600-900 one-time | 1 week | Lead capture from your website form into a CRM within 60 seconds, saving ~5 hrs/week of manual copy-paste; instant owner notification; basic appointment reminders | 50% upfront / 50% on delivery |
| **Growth** | $1,800-2,800 setup + $350-500/mo | 2-3 weeks | Starter, plus missed-call text-back that recovers ~15% of previously lost jobs, automated post-job review requests, monthly performance report | First month upfront, then net-15 |
| **Pro** | $3,500+ custom + $800-1,200/mo | 4+ weeks | Growth, plus multi-tech dispatch routing so no job sits unassigned, parts reorder alerts, AI-drafted follow-up emails | First month upfront, then net-15 |

**Value proposition per tier:**
- Starter: "Stop losing leads to a full inbox — every form fill lands in your CRM and pings you in under a minute."
- Growth: "Recover the ~15% of jobs you're losing to missed calls, and start collecting reviews automatically."
- Pro: "Run dispatch and inventory on autopilot so nothing falls through the cracks as you scale."

**Market check:** Searched Upwork/Fiverr "n8n automation" gigs and 3 local automation-agency sites — comparable one-off setups ran $500-4,000 and retainers $250-1,500/mo, so this table sits squarely in range, not wildly high or low.
