# Day 76 Notes: Closing Clients

*This day's deliverable is written scripts and templates, not an n8n workflow — see `DAILY_TASKS/DAY_76_Closing_Clients.md` for the full lesson.*

## Model Answer

### Discovery Call Script

**Opening/rapport:** "Thanks for making time — before I pitch anything, I want to understand how leads and calls actually flow through your business today."

**Discovery questions (5):**
1. Walk me through what happens today when a lead comes in after hours.
2. How many missed calls would you guess you get in a week?
3. What's your current review-collection process, if any?
4. Who currently handles this manually, and how much of their time does it eat?
5. What's stopped you from automating this before now?

**Qualification:** Budget range confirmed against Day 73's Starter/Growth/Pro tiers; timeline — "how soon would you want this live?"

**Next steps:** "I'll send a one-page proposal by tomorrow with pricing and timeline — sound good?"

### Proposal Template (using Day 73 pricing)

Executive summary → Current-state problem (missed calls, slow follow-up) → Proposed solution (Growth tier: missed-call text-back + review automation) → Pricing: $1,800-2,800 setup + $350-500/mo → Timeline: 2-3 weeks → Terms: first month upfront, net-15 after.

### One-Page Contract Template

Parties (Lane Automation Co. and Client) — Scope of work (per proposal) — Payment terms (50/50 or first-month-upfront) — Timeline/deliverables — Termination clause (either party may terminate with 14 days' written notice; work completed to date is billable).

### Objection Responses

- **"Too expensive"** — "Most clients make this back in the first month from recovered jobs alone — want me to walk through the math for your call volume?"
- **"I need to think about it"** — "Totally fair, it's your business. What's the one thing that would make this a clear yes for you?"
- **"Can you do it faster/cheaper?"** — "I could cut scope to hit that price — here's exactly what would drop off the Starter tier if we did."

**Practiced:** ran the full discovery → proposal → close sequence out loud once against the Coastal Air HVAC mock scenario.
