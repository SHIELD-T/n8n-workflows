## Model Answer

### Day 1 Cold Email (sent to Coastal Air HVAC)

**Subject:** Quick question about Coastal Air's after-hours call handling

**Hook:** Noticed a few of your Google reviews mention slow callback times after hours — looks like a lot of calls come in when the office is closed.

**Value prop:** I build simple automations that text customers back within 60 seconds of a missed call, so you stop losing jobs to whichever competitor calls back first.

**CTA:** Worth a free 15-minute call this week to see if it'd fit your setup?

---

**LinkedIn version (shorter/casual):** "Hey [Name] — saw Coastal Air is growing fast. Quick idea that could save your team a few hours a week on callback/scheduling — open to a quick chat?"

**Follow-up 1 (3 days later):** Quick bump + new value: "Just circling back — one stat that might be useful: missed-call text-back alone recovers ~15% of jobs that would otherwise go to voicemail."

**Follow-up 2 (1 week later):** Shorter, lower-commitment: "No worries if timing's off — happy to send a free 5-minute audit of your current lead flow instead, no call needed."

**Sent today:** to 3 real contacts — Coastal Air HVAC, QuickFix Plumbing, Home Comfort Heating & Cooling.
