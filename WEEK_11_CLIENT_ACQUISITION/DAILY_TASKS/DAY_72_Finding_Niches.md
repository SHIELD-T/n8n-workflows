# DAY 72: MONDAY - Finding Niches That Need Automation
**Week:** 11 — Client Acquisition  |  **Time:** 2-3 hours  |  **Difficulty:** Intermediate

## 🎯 What You'll Learn
- How to spot automation opportunities by looking at a business's repetitive, error-prone processes
- How to validate that a niche's pain points are real and worth paying to solve, not assumed
- How to size a market roughly (demand, competition, willingness to pay) before committing time to it
- Why "everyone" is not a target market, and how a narrow niche actually sells faster

Pay attention to: how the instructor frames automation opportunities in terms of a business owner's time and money, not in terms of n8n features.

## 🛠️ Build It: Step-by-Step
1. Open a blank doc and list 10 local or online businesses you could realistically research today (not hypothetical — actual named businesses).
2. For 5 of them, write down one specific repetitive task you believe they do manually (e.g., "manually copying form leads into a spreadsheet," "manually sending appointment reminders").
3. For each of those 5, do 15 minutes of real research: check their website, Google reviews, or social media for evidence of the pain point (e.g., complaints about slow response times, job postings for "data entry," a lack of online booking).
4. Narrow your list to 5 niches with the strongest evidence, and for each write: the pain point, the automation opportunity, a rough market size (how many similar businesses exist in a reachable radius/segment), and your best guess at competition level.
5. Rank your 5 niches by "profit potential vs. effort to reach" and pick your top 1-2 to focus on for the rest of this week's outreach work.
6. If you already have an industry track from earlier in the course, cross-check it against today's research — does the evidence support sticking with that track, or does today's research suggest a better-fitting niche?

## 🔑 Credentials Needed
- None — this is a research and writing day, no n8n build required

## ✅ Definition of Done
- [ ] 5 real, named businesses researched with actual evidence (not assumptions) of an automation pain point
- [ ] A written pain-point + opportunity + market-size note for each of the 5
- [ ] A ranked top 1-2 niche chosen to pursue for the rest of Week 11
- [ ] Your chosen niche is specific enough to write a targeted cold email about tomorrow — not "small businesses"

## 🐛 Common Pitfalls
- Picking a niche because it sounds impressive rather than because you found real evidence of pain
- Researching businesses that are too large (they likely have in-house solutions already) or too small (no budget for automation)
- Spreading research across 10+ industries instead of going deep on a focused shortlist — depth beats breadth here

## 🏭 Industry Track Application
Deep dive into your chosen industry track specifically today: identify 3-5 real businesses in that vertical (fintech, healthtech, EdTech, e-commerce, marketing, or logistics) and validate that automation pain points genuinely exist there before you build your whole Week 11 pitch around it.
