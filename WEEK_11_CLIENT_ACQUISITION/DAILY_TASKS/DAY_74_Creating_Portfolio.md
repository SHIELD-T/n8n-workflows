# DAY 74: WEDNESDAY - Creating a Portfolio
**Week:** 11 — Client Acquisition  |  **Time:** 2-3 hours  |  **Difficulty:** Intermediate

## 🎯 What You'll Learn
- How to turn a workflow you already built into a client-facing case study
- What a portfolio needs even before you have a single paying client
- How to write performance claims you can actually back up (or clearly label as illustrative)
- How to structure a portfolio so a prospect can scan it in under 60 seconds

## 🎥 Watch First
**Watch:** [What I Wish I Had Known Before Building 100+ n8n Workflows](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VB0ANci--Dc) - Building your automation portfolio section

Pay attention to: how the creator frames each workflow example around a business problem solved, not a technical walkthrough.

## 🛠️ Build It: Step-by-Step
1. Choose a free, fast platform for your portfolio today — a Notion page, a simple GitHub Pages site, or even a well-formatted PDF/Google Doc all count. Don't spend today building custom web infrastructure.
2. Pull 5 workflows from your Weeks 9-10 builds (or earlier weeks) that best demonstrate range: pick ones covering different problem types (lead capture, notifications, data sync, AI-powered processing, a complete system).
3. For each of the 5, write a short showcase entry: workflow name, the business problem it solves, the tools/platforms used, and one sentence on the outcome it would deliver for a client.
4. Write 1-2 case studies using a realistic scenario grounded in your actual work this course (be honest about what's a real client vs. a training project — label training projects as such, e.g., "training project demonstrating X capability").
5. Add your Day 73 pricing table to the portfolio as a "Services" section.
6. Add clear contact information and a call-to-action ("book a free 15-minute automation audit," or similar).
7. Proofread the whole thing for typos and broken links, then share the link with 2 people (classmates, friends) for a quick sanity check before you start outreach tomorrow.

## 🔑 Credentials Needed
- A free account on whichever platform you choose (Notion, GitHub, Google Docs, etc.)

## ✅ Definition of Done
- [ ] A published, shareable portfolio (a real URL or shareable doc link, not just a local file)
- [ ] 5 workflow showcases with problem, tools, and outcome for each
- [ ] At least 1 case study, honestly labeled as real client work or a training project
- [ ] Your Day 73 pricing table and contact info/CTA are included
- [ ] At least one other person reviewed it before you move on

## 🐛 Common Pitfalls
- Inventing specific client results/testimonials you can't back up — label training projects as training projects; fabricated "client" quotes will backfire if a prospect asks about them
- Over-investing time in platform/design polish today instead of getting a functional, honest portfolio live
- Writing showcases in pure technical jargon (node names, JSON) instead of business language a prospect would understand

## 🏭 Industry Track Application
Build a portfolio that showcases your industry automation expertise specifically — lead with your Day 72/73 niche work, even if you only have training-project examples so far. A portfolio clearly aimed at "automation for [your industry]" will convert better with a targeted prospect than a generic "I automate anything" page.
