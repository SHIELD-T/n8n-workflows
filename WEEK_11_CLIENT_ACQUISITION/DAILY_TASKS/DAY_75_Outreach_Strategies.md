# DAY 75: THURSDAY - Outreach Strategies
**Week:** 11 — Client Acquisition  |  **Time:** 2-3 hours  |  **Difficulty:** Intermediate

## 🎯 What You'll Learn
- How to write a cold outreach message that leads with the prospect's problem, not your service
- Why personalization (referencing something specific about the business) beats a generic template
- How to structure a short follow-up sequence without being pushy
- Which channel (email, LinkedIn, community post) fits which type of prospect

## 🎥 Watch First
**Watch:** [How I 100% Automated Long Form Content with n8n](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lF2bvXoV-Zg) - Client outreach strategies section

Pay attention to: how short and specific effective outreach messages are — resist the urge to explain everything you can do in the first message.

## 🛠️ Build It: Step-by-Step
1. Draft one cold outreach email using this structure: **Subject** (short, specific, no clickbait) / **Hook** (something specific you noticed about their business) / **Value prop** (one sentence on the time/money automation could save them) / **CTA** (a low-commitment ask, like a 15-minute call).
2. Write the email targeting one of the real businesses from your Day 72 list — use their actual name and a real detail you found in your research.
3. Repeat the same structure for a LinkedIn connection/message version (shorter, more casual) and a version suited to a community/forum post if your niche has one (e.g., a local Facebook business group).
4. Write a 2-message follow-up sequence (3 days later, 1 week later) for the email channel — each shorter than the last, each offering something new (a tip, a resource) rather than just "just following up."
5. Send your Day 1 email to 3 real contacts from your Day 72 list, or — if you're not ready to contact real businesses yet — post your pitch in the course community for peer feedback before sending it for real.
6. Track what you sent (recipient, channel, date, message) in a simple spreadsheet or Airtable so you can follow up on schedule.
7. If you get any reply today, note it and adjust tomorrow's Day 76 pitch based on the real objection or question raised.

## 🔑 Credentials Needed
- Email account you're comfortable sending from
- LinkedIn account (optional but recommended)

## ✅ Definition of Done
- [ ] One complete cold email drafted with subject/hook/value prop/CTA structure
- [ ] A LinkedIn version and (if applicable) a community-post version of the same pitch
- [ ] A 2-message follow-up sequence written
- [ ] The email was sent to 3 real contacts, or posted to the course community for feedback if you're not ready to contact real prospects yet
- [ ] A tracking log exists showing who you contacted, when, and via what channel

## 🐛 Common Pitfalls
- Leading with your services/credentials instead of their problem — prospects skim past self-focused openers
- Sending the exact same generic template to everyone — even light personalization dramatically improves response rates
- No tracking system — without a log you'll lose track of who needs a follow-up and when

## 🏭 Industry Track Application
Build automated outreach systems to find and connect with potential clients in your industry — but today, write the human message first. Tailor your hook and value prop to language your niche actually uses (a healthtech prospect responds to "reduce no-shows," a logistics prospect to "cut dispatch delays").
