## 🛠️ Build It: Step-by-Step
1. Pick your single best workflow from the course so far (any week). Rebuild it as a genuine template: rename generic values to `{{PLACEHOLDER}}` style variables, add a Sticky Note at the top explaining what to change before reusing it, and re-export the JSON.
2. Import `WEEK_12_SCALING/EXAMPLES/workflow_template_management_system.json` and compare — does your template follow the same conventions (clear naming, sticky-note instructions, no hardcoded secrets)?
3. Write a one-page "role card" for the first hire you'd realistically make (a VA, a junior automation builder, a sales-only person): what they'd own, what tools/credentials they'd need access to, and what stays with you.
4. Import `WEEK_12_SCALING/EXAMPLES/team_management_hiring_system.json` and `WEEK_12_SCALING/EXAMPLES/aaas_service_management_system.json`, confirm they run, and note which parts of your own business (from Week 11) could realistically plug into each.
5. Draw (on paper or in a doc — no special tool required) a one-page diagram: Client → Automation Templates → Team → AaaS Pricing Tier, showing how the four pieces connect for your specific business.

## 🐛 Common Pitfalls
- **"Template" that's really just the original workflow with a new name** — if someone else couldn't run it without asking you five questions first, it's not a template yet. The sticky-note instructions are the actual deliverable, not the workflow logic.
- **Hiring plans with no defined boundary** — "they'll help with automation" isn't a role. If you can't say what they're explicitly NOT allowed to touch (credentials, client billing, etc.), the role card isn't finished.
- **Skipping the diagram because it feels unnecessary** — it's the fastest way to notice a gap (e.g. you have templates and pricing but no actual handoff process to a hire).

## 🏭 Industry Track Application
Make the role card and diagram specific to your industry track's workflow — a Logistics automation template hands off differently than a Marketing one. Name the actual tools (e.g. "access to the client's shipping API credentials" vs. "access to the client's ad account") your hire would need.
