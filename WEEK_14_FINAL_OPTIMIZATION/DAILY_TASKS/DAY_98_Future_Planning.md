# DAY 98: Future Planning
**Week:** 14 — Final Optimization  |  **Time:** 3-4 hours  |  **Difficulty:** Advanced

## 🎯 What You'll Learn
- How to write a realistic 12-month plan instead of a 5-year fantasy with no near-term anchor
- How to build one real n8n workflow that tracks progress against your goals automatically, instead of checking in "someday"
- How to plan for the most likely scenario while still having a real fallback if growth is slower than hoped

## 🎥 Watch First
[What I Wish I Had Known Before Building 100+ n8n Workflows](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VB0ANci--Dc)
Watch for what the builder wishes they'd planned for earlier — use that as direct input into your own plan today.

## 🛠️ Build It: Step-by-Step
1. Write 3 real, specific 12-month goals (not "grow the business" — "reach $X MRR," "sign N clients in [industry track]," "hire 1 person for [role from Day 80]").
2. For each goal, write the single metric that would prove you hit it, and where that metric currently stands today (a real number, even if it's zero).
3. Build an n8n workflow with a **Schedule Trigger** (monthly) that reads your real metrics source (a Google Sheet you maintain, or your AaaS revenue tracker from Week 12) and compares current value against your 12-month target, computing percent-to-goal.
4. Add a **Slack**/**Email** node that sends yourself this monthly progress check automatically — test it once with real or realistic current data.
5. Write two brief scenarios: a "steady" plan (what you do if progress tracks roughly on schedule) and a "behind schedule" plan (one real fallback action per goal if you're at 50% of target by month 6) — both actionable, not vague reassurance.
6. Pick the single next action you'll take this week toward your top goal, and actually schedule it (a calendar event, a task in your tracker) — not just a note that you should.

## 🔑 Credentials Needed
Google Sheets/Airtable credential for the metrics source; Slack or Email credential for the monthly check-in.

## ✅ Definition of Done
- [ ] You have 3 specific, measurable 12-month goals with a real current baseline for each
- [ ] Your monthly progress-check workflow is built and was tested with real or realistic data
- [ ] You wrote a genuine "behind schedule" fallback per goal, not just an optimistic plan
- [ ] You scheduled one real, concrete next action for this week

## 🐛 Common Pitfalls
- **5-year visions with no near-term milestone** — a distant vision with no 12-month or 90-day anchor rarely gets acted on; keep this exercise grounded in the next year, concretely.
- **Progress-check workflow built but never tested** — if you haven't run it once with real data and actually received the message, you don't know it works.
- **No fallback plan, just optimism** — "we'll figure it out if we're behind" isn't a plan; write the specific action you'd actually take.

## 🏭 Industry Track Application
Your 3 goals should be denominated in your industry track's real unit of success (clients signed in that vertical, workflows deployed for that niche) rather than generic revenue-only targets, so progress tracking stays tied to the actual business you're building.
